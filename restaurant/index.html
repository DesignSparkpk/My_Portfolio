Our story

Two brothers, one passion for authentic flavor

Welcome to Al-Arabian Mandi & Shinwari House! Founded by two brothers with a passion for authentic flavors, we are proud to bring the rich tradition of Arabic Mandi and Shinwari cuisine to Haripur for the first time.

Our dishes are crafted with the finest ingredients, slow-cooked to perfection, and infused with the essence of Arabian and Pashtun heritage. Join us and savor the taste of authenticity!
