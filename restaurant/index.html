Our Story

Welcome to Al-Arabian Mandi & Shinwari House! Founded by two brothers with a passion for authentic flavors, we are proud to bring the rich tradition of Arabic Mandi and Shinwari cuisine to Haripur for the first time.

Our dishes are crafted with the finest ingredients, slow-cooked to perfection, and infused with the essence of Arabian and Pashtun heritage. Whether you're craving the aromatic flavors of Mandi or the bold taste of Shinwari, we promise an unforgettable dining experience that stays true to its roots.

Join us and savor the taste of authenticity!
